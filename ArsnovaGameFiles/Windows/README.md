Controls
    - WASD and space
        - Hold S while airborne to fall faster
    - LMB For Magic missile
    - RMB for fireball/bolt
    - Hold E while grounded to syphon mana back into your mana pool

Goals
    - Survive as long as possible and collect xp for score
